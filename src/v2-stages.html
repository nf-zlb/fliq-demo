Should 👶 under-16s be ❌ banned from social media? 📵

Governments are weighing laws that would bar under-16s from holding a social-media account, require platforms to verify age, and close the accounts of anyone younger.

Supporters say the years between 11 and 15 are when a person's sense of self is still forming. Critics say a ban simply pushes young people onto quieter, less-moderated corners of the web.

The short version: lawmakers want to stop under-16s from holding an account, ask platforms to verify age, and close the accounts of anyone younger. It's a big change — and it isn't settled yet.

Supporters point to the years between 11 and 15, when your sense of self is still taking shape. Critics worry the ban wouldn't remove the risk so much as move it somewhere quieter and harder to see.

Governments want a new rule. If you're under 16, you couldn't have an account — and apps would have to verify age and close accounts that are too young.

Some people think it's smart, because 11 to 15 is when you're still working out who you are. Others worry that kids would just move to quieter apps that are harder to keep safe.
